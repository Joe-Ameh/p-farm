About Dangote Group

Discover how a small trading enterprise founded in 1978 transformed into Sub-Saharan Africa's largest multi-industry conglomerate, pioneering self-reliance across the continent.

Who We Are

Dangote Industries Limited is a diversified conglomerate, providing essential daily needs to over 1.4 billion people across Africa. Headquartered in Lagos, Nigeria, our investments span oil refining, petrochemicals, cement, sugar, salt, fertilizer, automotive, packaging, and logistics.

Driven by a commitment to local value creation, Dangote Group produces what Africa consumes and consumes what Africa produces—reducing import dependence and creating tens of thousands of skilled jobs.

1978

Trading Company Founded in Kano & Lagos

Aliko Dangote establishes the Dangote Trading Company as a commodity trading business specializing in rice, sugar, and cement distribution.

1990s

Shift from Trading to Manufacturing

Dangote Group transitions from purely importing commodities to local manufacturing, establishing sugar refining and salt processing facilities in Lagos port terminals.

2000s

Commissioning Obajana & Listed Entities

Commissioning of the world-class Obajana Cement Plant in Kogi State, establishing Dangote Cement as Nigeria's manufacturing flagship and listing entities on the Nigerian Stock Exchange (NGX).

2010s

Pan-African Cement & Agro Sacks Expansion

Expanding cement production across 10 additional African countries (Ethiopia, Zambia, Tanzania, Senegal, South Africa) with total capacity exceeding 50 Million MT.

2020s

700,000 BPD Petroleum Refinery & Fertilizer Complex

Commissioning the world's largest single-train 700,000 BPD oil refinery and 3.0 Million MT Urea fertilizer complex in Lekki Free Zone, Lagos.
